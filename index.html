About the Conference

The CEPR-JIE Conference on Applied Industrial Organization is an annual conference organized in collaboration with the Centre for Economic Policy Research (CEPR) and the Journal of Industrial Economics. The 26th edition is hosted by LUISS University in Rome and funded by the European Research Council (ERC).

Accommodation & Directions to LUISS

Accommodation for participants will be arranged across two nearby hotels: Hotel Claridge Hilton (Viale Liegi 62) and Hotel Buenos Aires (Via Clitunno 9), both within short distance of the venue.

Hotel Claridge Hilton — Viale Liegi 62, 00198 Roma

From Termini Station (Piazzale dei Cinquecento): Take bus 910 or 223 and get off at Antonelli/Mercalli or Santiago del Cile. The hotel is within walking distance from the stop.

To LUISS (Viale Romania 32): approx. 15–20 min walk, or by bus lines 52, 53, 910.

Hotel Buenos Aires — Via Clitunno 9, 00198 Roma

From Termini Station (Piazzale dei Cinquecento): Take bus 92 or 38 and get off at Via Tagliamento/Via Clitunno or Trieste/Trento.

To LUISS (Viale Romania 32): approx. 15 min walk, or by bus lines 38, 52.

Public Transport in Rome

A single ticket costs €1.50 and is valid for 100 minutes from first validation. Tickets can be purchased via contactless payment (tap & go) directly on the bus, at tabacchi shops, newsstands, or the ATAC ticket office at Piazzale dei Cinquecento. Tickets must be validated upon boarding using the machines located near the doors.

Conference Dinner

The dinner location is a short walk which goes through Villa Borghese. It can also be reached by tram 3 from Piazza Ungheria to the terminal, followed by a 2-minute walk.
